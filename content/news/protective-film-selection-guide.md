  en:
    - "Most quality complaints trace back to one root cause: the film was specified before the application was fully understood. Buyers usually start from price per square meter; engineers should start from the surface. This guide gives you the seven questions we ask every customer before recommending a model."
    - "**1. What is the substrate?** Aluminum profiles, stainless steel sheets, coated metal, PC/PMMA sheets, glass and painted surfaces have very different surface energies. Smooth, high-energy surfaces hold low-adhesion film well; rough or low-energy surfaces need more adhesive. Tell us the exact material and finish (brushed, anodized, mirror, powder-coated) — this single detail eliminates most wrong picks."
    - "**2. What processing follows?** Bending, punching, laser cutting, CNC milling and deep drawing all add heat, pressure and mechanical stress. If you bend after lamination, the film needs elongation matching the sheet. If you laser-cut, ask for a film rated for that process — otherwise burnt edges and residue follow."
    - "**3. Adhesion window.** Acrylic PET films typically span 5 to 1,500 g/in. Low tack (5–50 g/in) suits short-term protection on smooth surfaces; medium (50–300 g/in) is the workhorse for aluminum profiles and stainless transport; high (300–1,500 g/in) is for rough surfaces, thick gauge, long storage or outdoor exposure."
    - "**4. Film thickness.** Thicker film resists puncture and tearing during handling, but costs more and is stiffer to apply. Common choices: 30–50 µm for light-duty indoor protection, 50–70 µm for profiles and sheet metal, 80–120 µm plus for transport with rough handling or sharp edges."
    - "**5. Temperature.** Standard acrylic adhesives are usually fine up to about 60–80 °C; rubber-based systems lose cohesion earlier; silicone systems handle high heat but cost more and can leave silicone contamination. Always state peak temperature *and* dwell time at that temperature."
    - "**6. Dwell time and environment.** A film that peels cleanly after two weeks may bond much harder after three months in a hot warehouse. UV exposure, humidity, salt air during sea freight and stacking pressure all change the effective peel strength. Tell us the target removal date, not just an optimistic 'as soon as possible'."
    - "**7. Removal conditions.** Who removes it, by hand or machine, and at what temperature? Hand removal on site in winter behaves very differently from a warm line. If the film must be removed by automated equipment, ask us to match release characteristics to your unwind speed."
    - "**Recommended starting points.** For aluminum profiles: our PE self-adhesive series (GDY-025, GDY-033, GDY-047, GDY-032) covers most extrusion finishes. For electronics and die-cutting: PET series models GDY-024, GDY-011, GDY-008 and GDY-060. Send us your substrate and process conditions and we will send free samples for a trial on your line."

    - "**2. 后续还要什么加工？** 折弯、冲压、激光切割、CNC 铣削、深拉都会带来热量、压力和机械应力。若覆膜后还要折弯，薄膜的伸长率必须匹配板材；若激光切割，必须选用适用该工艺的膜，否则会出现烧边和残胶。"
    - "**3. 粘性窗口。** 丙烯酸 PET 膜通常在 5–1500 g/in 区间。低粘（5–50 g/in）适合光滑表面的短期保护；中粘（50–300 g/in）是铝型材与不锈钢运输的主力；高粘（300–1500 g/in）用于粗糙表面、厚板、长期仓储或户外暴露。"
    - "**4. 薄膜厚度。** 厚膜更抗穿刺、搬运不易破，但单价更高、贴合更挺不易服帖。常见选择：30–50 μm 用于室内轻保护；50–70 μm 用于型材与钣金；80–120 μm 及以上用于粗暴搬运或有锐边的运输。"
    - "**5. 温度。** 常规丙烯酸胶一般可耐 60–80 ℃；橡胶系胶更早失去内聚；硅胶系耐高温但成本高，且可能造成硅污染。请务必同时说明峰值温度**和**该温度下的持续时间。"
    - "**6. 贴覆时长与环境。** 两周内能干净剥离的膜，在高温仓库里放三个月可能粘得很死。紫外线、湿度、海运中的盐雾、堆叠压力都会改变实际剥离力。请给出目标剥离时间，而不是笼统的「越快越好」。"
    - "**7. 剥离条件。** 谁来撕？手工还是机器？在什么温度下？冬季现场手撕与温热产线上的表现完全不同。若需自动剥离，请告知放卷速度，我们会匹配对应的离型特性。"

    - "Sebagian besar keluhan kualitas berakar pada satu penyebab: model film ditentukan sebelum aplikasinya dipahami. Tim pembelian biasanya mulai dari harga per meter persegi; teknisi seharusnya mulai dari permukaan. Berikut tujuh pertanyaan yang selalu kami ajukan sebelum merekomendasikan model."
    - "**1. Apa substratnya?** Profil aluminium, lembaran stainless, logam berlapis, lembar PC/PMMA, kaca, dan permukaan cat memiliki energi permukaan sangat berbeda. Permukaan halus berenergi tinggi menahan film adesi rendah dengan baik; permukaan kasar atau berenergi rendah butuh perekat lebih. Beri tahu kami material dan finishing pastinya (brushed, anodized, cermin, powder coating) — satu detail ini menghilangkan sebagian besar kesalahan pilih."
    - "**2. Proses setelahnya apa?** Pembengkokan, pelubangan, pemotongan laser, milling CNC, dan deep drawing menambah panas, tekanan, dan tegangan mekanis. Jika ditekuk setelah dilaminasi, film perlu elongasi yang cocok. Jika dipotong laser, minta film yang sesuai proses itu — kalau tidak, akan muncul tepi terbakar dan residu."
    - "**3. Jendela adesi.** Film PET akrilik biasanya 5 hingga 1.500 g/in. Adesi rendah (5–50 g/in) untuk perlindungan jangka pendek di permukaan halus; menengah (50–300 g/in) andalan untuk profil aluminium dan pengangkutan stainless; tinggi (300–1.500 g/in) untuk permukaan kasar, lembar tebal, penyimpanan lama, atau luar ruangan."
    - "**4. Ketebalan film.** Film tebal lebih tahan tusukan dan sobek saat penanganan, tapi lebih mahal dan lebih kaku saat diaplikasikan. Pilihan umum: 30–50 µm untuk proteksi ringan indoor; 50–70 µm untuk profil dan lembar logam; 80–120 µm ke atas untuk transportasi kasar atau tepi tajam."
    - "**5. Suhu.** Perekat akrilik standar umumnya baik hingga sekitar 60–80 °C; sistem karet lebih cepat kehilangan kohesi; sistem silikon tahan panas tinggi tetapi lebih mahal dan berisiko kontaminasi silikon. Selalu sebut suhu puncak *dan* durasi pada suhu tersebut."
    - "**6. Waktu tempel dan lingkungan.** Film yang terkelupas bersih setelah dua minggu bisa menempel jauh lebih kuat setelah tiga bulan di gudang panas. UV, kelembapan, udara asin saat pengiriman laut, dan tekanan tumpukan semua mengubah kekuatan kelupas efektif. Beri tahu tanggal target pelepasan, bukan sekadar 'secepatnya'."
    - "**7. Kondisi pelepasan.** Siapa yang melepas, manual atau mesin, pada suhu berapa? Pelepasan manual di musim dingin sangat berbeda dengan line yang hangat. Jika dilepas otomatis, beri tahu kecepatan unwind agar kami menyesuaikan karakteristik release."
    - "**Titik awal yang direkomendasikan.** Profil aluminium: seri PE self-adhesive GDY-025, GDY-033, GDY-047, GDY-032 mencakup sebagian besar finishing ekstrusi.
