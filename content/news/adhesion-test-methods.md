  en:
    - "Two suppliers can quote the same '300 g/in' and mean completely different things. Adhesion is not a material constant — it is a measurement that depends on speed, angle, temperature, dwell time and substrate. If you want to compare offers honestly, ask which test standard and conditions produced the number."
    - "**The 180° peel test.** The most common method for protective films: laminate the film onto a standard test panel (usually stainless steel or glass), condition it for a defined time, then peel it back at 180° at a fixed crosshead speed (commonly 300 mm/min) and record the average force in g/in or N/25 mm. Peel force usually stabilises after the first few centimetres, so the initial spike is discarded."
    - "**Relevant standards.** For pressure-sensitive tapes the widely used references are **GB/T 2792** (China) and **ASTM D3330** (international), which specify panel preparation, roller weight, dwell time and peel geometry. When you ask any supplier for adhesion data, ask them to state the standard, peel speed and dwell time along with the value."
    - "**90° and T-peel variants.** Some applications — curved profiles, films that are removed by folding back sharply — correlate better with 90° peel measurement. If removal happens by machine at high speed, ask for data measured at a speed closer to your line, because peel force rises with peel rate for most acrylic systems."
    - "**Conditioning matters.** A 20-minute dwell gives a very different result from a 24-hour dwell, and a 24-hour dwell at 23 °C differs from one at 60 °C. Realistic comparison means testing under conditions that match your actual storage: same temperature, same dwell time, same panel material."
    - "**What we measure per batch.** For every production lot we record: peel strength before and after heat ageing, thickness, tensile strength and elongation, plus a visual check for coating uniformity. Batch records are kept so a shipped roll can be traced back to its raw materials and test data. Third-party testing (SGS / RoHS / REACH) can be arranged per order requirements."
    - "**How to run your own check.** You do not need a laboratory to catch obvious mismatches. Laminate a sample on your real substrate, leave it for your real dwell time under your real conditions, then peel by hand and look at three things: resistance you feel, whether the film stretches or tears, and whether any adhesive stays behind."
    - "**Interpreting results.** Clean removal with no residue and moderate initial force means the adhesion is in range. If the film lifts at the edges or falls off during transport, you need a higher tack grade. If adhesive transfers to the panel or removal needs solvent, you need lower tack or a different adhesive system."
    - "Ask us for a sample roll of two or three adjacent grades and run this check side by side — it takes a week and removes most of the guesswork from a long-term specification. We will send test reports for the same production lot on request."

    - "两家供应商报同一个「300 g/in」，含义可能完全不同。粘着力不是材料常数，而是一个受速度、角度、温度、贴覆时长与基材影响的测量值。想客观对比报价，先问这个数字出自哪个标准与条件。"
    - "**180° 剥离测试。** 保护膜最常用的方法：将薄膜贴到标准试板（通常不锈钢板或玻璃），按规定时间停放，然后在固定速度（常见 300 mm/min）下以 180° 回拉，记录平均力值，单位 g/in 或 N/25mm。通常前几厘米的力值不稳会舍弃，取平稳段的平均值。"
    - "**相关标准。** 压敏胶带领域常用的参照是**GB/T 2792**（中国）与**ASTM D3330**（国际），二者规定了试板处理、压辊重量、停放时间与剥离几何。向任何供应商索取粘性数据时，请同时要求注明标准、剥离速度与停放时间。"
    - "**90° 与 T 型剥离。** 某些场景——曲面型材、需要大角度折回撕膜的场合——与 90° 剥离测试的相关性更好。如果是机器高速撕膜，请索要接近你实际线速下测得的数据，因为多数丙烯酸体系剥离力随剥离速度上升。"
    - "**停放条件是关键。** 停放 20 分钟与 24 小时结果差别很大；23 ℃ 下 24 小时与 60 ℃ 下 24 小时又不一样。有意义的对比，必须在与你实际仓储相同的条件下测试——同温度、同时长、同试板材质。"
    - "**我们每批测什么。** 每批生产我们记录：热老化前后的剥离强度、厚度、拉伸强度与伸长率，以及涂布均匀性目检。批次记录会留存，任何出货卷都可追溯到原料与检测数据；也可按订单要求安排第三方检测（SGS / RoHS / REACH）。"
    - "**你自己也能做的检查。** 不必进实验室就能筛掉明显不匹配的型号：把样品贴到你的真实基材上，按你的真实时长与条件停放，然后手撕，看三点——手感阻力、薄膜是否被拉长或撕断、板上是否留胶。"
    - "**结果怎么读。** 剥离干净、无残胶且初始力度适中，说明粘性区间合适。若运输中翘边或脱落，需要更高粘型号；若胶转移到试板上或需要用溶剂才能擦净，则需要更低的粘性或更换胶系。"
    - "建议同时索取两三个相邻等级的样品卷，并排做一次上述检查——花一周时间，就能把长期选型里的试错基本消除。同一生产批次的检测报告可应要求提供。"

    - "**Chúng tôi đo gì mỗi lô.** Với mỗi lô sản xuất chúng tôi ghi: lực bóc trước và sau lão hóa nhiệt, độ dày, độ bền kéo và độ giãn dài, cùng kiểm tra trực quan độ đồng đều lớp phủ. Hồ sơ lô được lưu để bất kỳ cuộn đã giao đều truy xuất được nguyên liệu và dữ liệu thử; thử nghiệm bên thứ ba (SGS / RoHS / REACH) có thể sắp xếp theo yêu cầu đơn hàng."

    - "Dua pemasok bisa menawarkan angka '300 g/in' yang sama tetapi maksudnya sangat berbeda. Adesi bukan konstanta material — ia adalah hasil pengukuran yang bergantung pada kecepatan, sudut, suhu, waktu tempel, dan substrat. Untuk membandingkan penawaran secara jujur, tanyakan standar dan kondisi apa yang menghasilkan angka tersebut."
    - "**Uji kelupas 180°.** Metode paling umum untuk film pelindung: tempelkan film ke panel uji standar (biasanya stainless atau kaca), biarkan sesuai waktu yang ditentukan, lalu kelupas pada 180° dengan kecepatan tetap (umumnya 300 mm/menit) dan catat gaya rata-rata dalam g/in atau N/25 mm. Gaya kelupas biasanya stabil setelah beberapa sentimeter pertama, sehingga lonjakan awal dibuang."
    - "**Standar terkait.** Untuk perekat peka tekan, referensi yang banyak digunakan adalah **GB/T 2792** (Tiongkok) dan **ASTM D3330** (internasional), yang menetapkan persiapan panel, berat roller, waktu tempel, dan geometri kelupas. Saat meminta data adesi dari pemasok mana pun, minta agar mereka menyebut standar, kecepatan kelupas, dan waktu tempel bersama nilainya."
    - "**Varian 90° dan T-peel.** Beberapa aplikasi — profil lengkung, film yang dilepas dengan melipat tajam — lebih berkorelasi dengan pengukuran kelupas 90°. Jika pelepasan dilakukan mesin berkecepatan tinggi, minta data yang diukur mendekati kecepatan line Anda, karena gaya kelupas naik seiring laju kelupas pada sebagian besar sistem akrilik."
    - "**Kondisioning penting.** Waktu diam 20 menit memberi hasil sangat berbeda dari 24 jam, dan 24 jam pada 23 °C berbeda dengan 60 °C. Perbandingan yang bermakna berarti menguji pada kondisi yang sesuai dengan penyimpanan aktual Anda: suhu sama, waktu sama, material panel sama."
    - "**Apa yang kami ukur per batch.** Untuk setiap lot produksi kami catat: kekuatan kelupas sebelum dan sesudah penuaan panas, ketebalan, kekuatan tarik dan elongasi, plus pemeriksaan visual keseragaman pelapisan.
