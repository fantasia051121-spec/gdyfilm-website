  en:
    - "The claim lands the same way every time: a coil of 304 that spent six weeks on the water arrives at a coastal fabricator with the film intact and edge to edge clean — except for a band of brown staining and pinhead pitting a few centimetres wide along the cut edges, exactly where moisture crept under the film during the voyage. The buyer's first question is why the film did not stop it, and the second is whether film is worth applying at all for export shipments near the coast. Both questions have useful answers, but only if you are clear about what a pressure-sensitive PE film is designed to do."
    - "Start with what film is for. A protective film is a soft PE carrier with a pressure-sensitive adhesive, and its job is mechanical: it stands between the polished surface and forklift edges, strapping, grit, dust and the stacking marks that come from six weeks of being moved. It is very good at that job. What it is not is a hermetic barrier against chemistry. PE transmits water vapour slowly rather than not at all, and every roll has film ends, trim edges and cut lines where the interface is open to the air."
    - "Now the other side of the pair. Stainless steel resists corrosion through a chromium-oxide passive layer that is self-repairing in clean, oxygenated conditions — and locally destroyed by chlorides. The commonly cited working figures are that 304 begins pitting in water with roughly **200 mg/L of chloride**, while 316 holds out to around **1000 mg/L**; in marine air the chloride arrives as deposited salt that dissolves into every film of condensation. Seawater itself runs about 3.5 percent salt, so a single evaporating drop at a cut edge concentrates far beyond any of those thresholds."
    - "This is why film can make salt staining worse, not better, when it is applied wrong. If the sheet is laminated over a wet, salty or fingerprinted surface, the adhesive seals the contamination against the metal with no route out. Moisture then moves through the PE and through the edges, sits at the interface, and the chloride keeps working in the dark, out of sight of inspection. The classic result is tea staining that is worst near film edges and cut lines — the pattern in the claim that opened this article."
    - "The test most buyers reach for does not settle the question, because it was never designed to. Neutral salt spray to **ISO 9227** (in China, GB/T 10125) runs a **5 percent NaCl** solution at **pH 6.5–7.2**, chamber temperature **35 °C**, with a collection rate of 1–2 mL per 80 cm² per hour, for durations chosen from 48, 96, 240, 480 or 720 hours. It is a comparative corrosion test for metals and coatings — rating a steel grade, a passivation treatment or a coating system against another. A PE protective film is not the product under test, and treating a film as a salt-spray barrier has no standard behind it."
    - "So does film help against salt? It helps against everything that accompanies the salt. On a documented export route the film prevents strap abrasion, edge dings, dust burnishing and handling scratches through transit, warehouse and fabrication — damage that is certain, while corrosion is conditional. It does nothing to stop chloride attack, and if it is applied over contamination it actively hides the attack until the film comes off. That is the honest division of labour, and every purchasing decision about export film should start from it."
    - "The practical rules follow directly. Laminate only clean, dry surfaces — film over condensation is a sealed corrosion cell. Use a grade whose adhesion suits the removal window, because adhesion climbs during long storage and a film that peels at **300 g/25 mm** per **GB/T 2792** when fresh can be a residue problem after a monsoon season in a hot yard. After any long sea leg, inspect at the edges first: lift a strip at the trim line and look for water marks before the coil goes into stock."
    - "Grade selection then becomes simple. For polished and brushed decorative stainless, keep to a low-adhesion PE grade and shorten the removal window rather than cranking adhesion up. For surfaces heading into chemical or wet processing, the conversation is different — that is the territory of films built for acid and alkali exposure rather than general surface protection, such as the [acid- and alkali-resistant film](/products/GDY-014) in our PET line. For general protection of stainless panels and formed parts in storage and transit, the standard reference is the [blue PE protective film](/products/GDY-031): the colour also makes edge lift and water marks obvious at incoming inspection, which is precisely where salt damage announces itself."
    - "Close the loop with a fixed routine instead of an assumption. On arrival: check edge lift and water marks, then stock or reject. Before any shipment longer than a fortnight: dry surfaces, full-edge lamination, and a documented removal date on the pack. Before releasing a new grade to the line: peel-test it on your own substrate to GB/T 2792 at 23 °C, and verify no residue after the realistic storage time you actually face. Every film supplier should support this with [free samples](/free-sample) — run the trial on your metal, in your yard, before the container is booked."
  zh:
    - "索赔函的内容每次都一样：一卷 304 在海上漂了六个星期，到港时膜完好无损、板面干干净净——唯独剪切边缘几厘米宽的范围出现棕色锈斑和针尖状点蚀，正好是航行途中水汽钻进膜下的位置。采购的第一个问题是膜为什么没挡住，第二个问题是出口海运到底还值不值得贴膜。两个问题都有明确答案，前提是先弄清压敏 PE 保护膜究竟是设计来做什么的。"
    - "先说膜的本职。保护膜是一层软质 PE 基材加压敏胶，承担的是机械防护：隔开抛光表面与叉车货叉、打包带、砂粒、粉尘，以及六周装卸搬运堆叠留下的压痕。这件事它做得非常好。但它不是对抗化学侵蚀的密封屏障——PE 会缓慢透湿而不是完全不透，而且每卷膜都有端面、切边和裁切线，这些位置的膜与金属界面是敞开着的。"
    - "再看不锈钢那一侧。不锈钢靠氧化铬钝化膜抗腐蚀，在洁净含氧环境下钝化膜能自修复，遇到氯离子则会被局部破坏。行业常用的经验数据是：水中氯离子达到约 **200 mg/L** 时 304 开始点蚀，316 大约能撑到 **1000 mg/L**；海洋大气里的氯以沉降盐分的形式存在，溶进每一层冷凝水。海水本身含盐约 3.5%，切边上任何一滴正在蒸发的海水，浓度都远超上述门槛。"
    - "所以贴法不对时，膜会让盐蚀更严重而不是更轻。如果板材带水、带盐、带指纹就直接覆膜，胶层等于把污染物封死在金属表面，没有任何出路。水分随后透过 PE 和边缘渗入，停在界面上，氯离子在检验看不见的黑暗界面里持续工作。典型结果就是茶渍状锈斑在膜的边缘和裁切线附近最严重——正是开头那封索赔函里的图案。"
    - "多数采购想到的测试也回答不了这个问题，因为它从来不是为此设计的。**ISO 9227** 中性盐雾试验（国内对应 GB/T 10125）用 **5% NaCl** 溶液、**pH 6.5~7.2**、箱温 **35℃**、收集量每小时每 80 cm² 一至二毫升，时长在 48、96、240、480、720 小时里按需选取。它是对金属和涂层体系的对比性腐蚀试验——评价钢种、钝化处理或涂层之间的相对表现。PE 保护膜不是被测对象，把膜当盐雾屏障看待没有任何标准依据。"
    - "那么膜对盐雾到底有没有用？它对付的是与盐伴生的一切。在可查证出口航线上，膜防的是运输、仓储、加工全程的打包带磨伤、磕边、灰尘擦毛和搬运划伤——这些损伤必然发生，而腐蚀只是有条件发生。它挡不住氯离子侵蚀；贴在有污染的表面上时，还会把侵蚀藏起来，直到撕膜才暴露。这就是膜在盐雾场景里诚实的分工，一切出口膜的采购决策都应该从这里出发。"
    - "实操规则由此直接推出。只在洁净干燥的表面覆膜——覆在冷凝水上的膜就是一座封闭的腐蚀电池。按撕膜窗口选粘力档位：长期存放中粘力会爬升，一批新膜按 **GB/T 2792** 测 **300 g/25 mm** 剥离力，在炎热场地放过一个雨季后就可能变成残胶难题。任何长途海运到港后，先查边缘：在切边处掀起一条膜，看有没有水痕，再决定入库还是退货。"
    - "选型于是变得简单。抛光和拉丝装饰不锈钢，用低粘力 PE 档位并缩短撕膜窗口，而不是一味调高粘力。进入化学或湿法加工的表面则是另一回事——那是耐酸碱膜的领地，不是通用保护膜能覆盖的，可参考 PET 线的[耐酸碱保护膜](/products/GDY-014)。不锈钢板和成型件在仓储运输中的通用防护，标准参照是[蓝色 PE 保护膜](/products/GDY-031)：亮蓝色让翘边和水痕在入库检验时一目了然，而盐蚀恰恰最先在这些位置现身。"
    - "最后用固定流程收尾，别靠假设。到货：查翘边和水痕，再定入库或拒收。任何超过两周的运输：表面干燥、整边覆膜、包装上写明预定撕膜日期。新档位上线前：用自己的基材按 GB/T 2792 在 23℃ 做剥离验证，并按你实际面对的存放时长确认无残胶。正规膜厂都应支持[免费样品](/free-sample)——订舱之前，先在自己的金属、自己的场地上把试验跑完。"

Kết quả kinh điển là vệt ố trà nặng nhất gần mép màng và đường cắt — đúng mẫu trong đơn khiếu nại mở đầu bài này."
