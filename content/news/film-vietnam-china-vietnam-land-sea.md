  en:
    - "Two orders, same film, same week. One leaves a Guangdong plant on a truck through the Friendship Pass border crossing and reaches a fabricator near Hanoi in three days. The other sails south toward Ho Chi Minh City and lands eighteen days later. Three months on, the trucked panels peel clean, while the shipped ones show adhesive drift at the edges. Nothing was wrong with the film itself — the two lanes simply stressed it in completely different ways."
    - "Start with the transit numbers, and treat them as typical ranges quoted by China-Vietnam line-haul forwarders rather than promises. Land trucking from Guangdong through border crossings such as Friendship Pass or Dongxing typically runs 2 to 4 days door to door into northern Vietnam — Hanoi, Hai Phong, Bac Ninh — about 4 to 6 days into the central provinces, and roughly 4 to 8 days into Ho Chi Minh City, Binh Duong and Dong Nai. Sea freight from southern Chinese ports is a port-to-port voyage of about 8 to 13 days, which becomes 10 to 18 days door to door for LCL once clearance and delivery are added."
    - "The variable that decides film performance is not distance. It is dwell time: how long the roll sits in contact with the panel, at temperature, under compression, before someone peels it. A trucked load reaches the customer with 3 to 8 days of contact history. A sea shipment carries 2 to 4 weeks of it, often in a container that sat on a sun-exposed deck for part of the voyage. Time and heat both push peel strength upward, so a grade that peels cleanly after a week on a truck can drift toward the top of its window after three weeks in a box."
    - "Heat inside a closed container is the second factor, and it compounds with dwell. Closed containers on tropical routes commonly see interior temperatures above 40 degrees Celsius during the day. The industry screening cycle for exactly this combination of heat and moisture is damp-heat conditioning at 40 degrees Celsius and 90 percent relative humidity for 72 hours, after which the film is peeled and inspected for residue. A film that passes that cycle on your substrate has margin for the sea lane; one tested only at room temperature does not tell you anything about it."
    - "Landed cost follows a different logic, because film is bulky relative to its weight. A roll of protective film can be 150 to 2100 mm wide and 200 to 1000 m long at only 0.02 to 0.20 mm thick, so one pallet fills a lot of volume while weighing very little. Land line-haul is typically quoted per kilogram or per cubic metre, sea LCL per cubic metre, and full-container per box. For light, bulky cargo such as film, a per-kilogram land quote often beats an LCL sea quote, while a full container of densely packed pallets is usually cheaper by sea than by truck into the south."
    - "The two lanes also damage cargo differently, and the failure fingerprints are distinct. Land freight involves several cross-dock touches — pickup, the forwarder warehouse, the border warehouse, a Vietnamese warehouse, final delivery — so the typical damage is mechanical: crushed edges, telescoped rolls, film scored before it ever touches a product. Sea freight involves few touches but long static compression in heat, so the typical damage is adhesive: peel drift, edge lift and residue where the film was tightest against the surface."
    - "This is why the right specification depends on the lane. For long sea dwell, pick a grade whose peel strength sits in the lower half of its window, or a co-extruded film that carries no adhesive at all — our PE three-layer co-extruded film runs 30 to 300 g/25mm peel, 0.02 to 0.20 mm thick, 150 to 2100 mm wide, and peels clean because the tack is built into the layers rather than coated on. [Full parameters are on the product page](/products/GDY-036). For land freight with heavy rehandling, a tougher carrier matters more than tack."
    - "Whatever the lane, blue PE film remains the standard reference for metal panels shipped into Vietnam, because the colour makes edge lift, water staining and scoring obvious at incoming inspection — problems get caught at the dock instead of at the press. [The blue PE grade is documented here](/products/GDY-031). On packing: stretch-wrap every pallet so rolls cannot breathe and shift, keep roll ends supported rather than overhanging, and leave original wrapping on until the moment of use, since unwrapped rolls are where both condensation and dust start."
    - "Before committing a lane, test it the way it will actually run. Order [free samples](/free-sample), apply them to your own panels, and split the test: keep one set in a dry room, and put the other through the worst leg — either a real shipment or the 40 degrees Celsius, 90 percent RH, 72-hour cycle — then peel both to GB/T 2792 and compare. The difference between the two numbers is what the lane costs you in film, and it is the only honest input for choosing between a lower-tack grade, a co-extruded one, or a different adhesive system altogether."
    - "The decision rule is simple enough to pin above a desk. Northern Vietnam, small or urgent lots: go by land, and spend attention on packing against rehandling. Southern Vietnam, full pallets, regular replenishment: go by sea, and spend attention on dwell — lower peel, cleaner adhesive, longer protection window. In both cases, tell your film supplier the exact lane, the expected dwell in days and the shipping month; a film chosen with the lane in mind costs the same as one chosen blind, and performs nothing like it."

    - "到岸成本是另一套逻辑，因为膜是一种「体积大、重量轻」的货。一卷保护膜幅宽可达 150~2100mm、卷长 200~1000m，厚度却只有 0.02~0.20mm，一个托盘占掉的体积很大，重量却很轻。陆运干线一般按公斤或按方报价，海运拼箱按方报价，整柜按柜报价。对膜这种轻抛货，陆运按公斤的报价经常低于海运拼箱；而码满托盘的整柜货，发南越走海运通常比整车陆运更省。"
    - "两条线路对货物的伤害方式也不同，损伤指纹很容易区分。陆运要经过多次中转装卸——提货、货代仓、口岸仓、越南仓、末端派送——所以典型损伤是机械性的：卷边压伤、卷材松脱、膜还没上产线就被划伤。海运接触次数少，但长时间静态受压加高温，典型损伤是胶性的：剥离力漂移、边缘翘起、贴得最紧的地方留残胶。"
    - "所以选膜要看线路。海运长停留，选剥离力落在窗口下半段的档位，或者干脆选不含胶的共挤膜——我们的 PE 三层共挤膜剥离力 30~300 g/25mm、厚度 0.02~0.20mm、幅宽 150~2100mm，粘性做在层与层之间而不是涂上去的，撕下来不留残胶。[完整参数在产品页](/products/GDY-036)。陆运反复装卸多，膜基材的抗冲击比粘性档位更重要。"
    - "无论走哪条线，发往越南的金属板材，蓝色 PE 膜仍是标准参照——颜色让翘边、水痕、划伤在入库检验时一眼可见，问题在码头就被拦下，而不是到冲床前才暴露。[蓝色 PE 膜的规格见这里](/products/GDY-031)。包装上：每个托盘都要缠膜到位，让卷材不能窜动；卷端要有支撑、不要悬空；原包装留到使用前一刻再拆，散卷恰恰是水汽和灰尘侵入的入口。"
    - "在锁定线路之前，按线路的真实工况做验证。先申请[免费样品](/free-sample)，贴在自己的板材上，然后分组：一组留在干燥房间，另一组走最严酷的一程——真实发一票货，或者做 40℃、90% 相对湿度、72 小时的湿热循环——最后两组都按 GB/T 2792 做剥离对比。两个数字的差值，就是这条线路在膜上收走的成本，也是你在低粘档位、共挤膜、不同胶水体系之间做选择时唯一诚实的依据。"
    - "决策规则简单到可以贴在案头。北越、小批量或急单：走陆运，把功夫花在防反复装卸的包装上。南越、整托盘、周期性补货：走海运，把功夫花在停留时间上——更低的剥离力、更干净的胶、更长的保护窗口。两种情况都一样：把准确线路、预计停留天数、发货月份告诉膜供应商。带着线路信息选的膜，和闭着眼睛选的膜价格相同，表现完全不同。"
